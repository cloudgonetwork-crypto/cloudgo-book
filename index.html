Restore from backup — or bulk-add past data

Restore from an Excel backup, or fill in a blank template with older shifts you already know about (from a previous app, Grab/Gojek's own trip history, or your own notes) and upload it here — much faster than typing each one into the app by hand.

Safe to re-import the same file as many times as you like — matching entries are updated in place, not duplicated.

Fix a stuck job total

If a shift's total looks wrong compared to your logged jobs, tap this to recalculate everything from scratch — safe to use anytime, doesn't create duplicates or lose data.

Connect Google Sheets

Push your data straight into a Google Sheet you own, using your Google account. You'll pick which sheet to use — the app only ever gets access to that one file, nothing else in your Drive.

Heads up: this connection is only active while this browser tab stays open — it resets whenever the page reloads (switching apps, restarting your phone, etc.), which happens often. For reliable auto-sync, the "Google Sheets export (script method)" card below is a better fit — it doesn't have this limitation.
